ADDING OR CHANGING PRODUCTS — MY WISH COUTURE
==============================================

EASIEST WAY: paste this to Claude and fill in the blanks

  "I'm updating my My Wish Couture website (shop.html).
   I want to [add / change / remove] this product:
   Name: ___  Fabric/style note: ___  Price: ___
   Category: everyday / formal / bridal / jewelry
   Photo file name (if any): ___
   Give me the exact code and tell me where to paste it."


DOING IT YOURSELF
In shop.html, every product sits between two comments:
  <!-- PRODUCT START: name -->   ...   <!-- PRODUCT END -->

To add one: copy one full block, paste it inside the right category,
then change:
  1. The name (in 3 spots: photo text, <h4>, data-item-name)
  2. The fabric note (<div class="note"> and data-item-description)
  3. The price (in 2 spots: <div class="price"> and data-item-price)
  4. data-item-id to a brand new unique word, like red-lawn-suit-2

To remove one: delete everything from its PRODUCT START to PRODUCT END.

ADDING A PHOTO
1. Upload the photo to GitHub (Add file > Upload files). Keep the name
   simple, no spaces, like red-lawn.jpg
2. In that product, replace this line:
     <div class="ph">... photo coming soon</div>
   with:
     <img src="red-lawn.jpg" alt="Red Lawn Suit">

CHANGING A PRICE
Change it in BOTH spots in shop.html (the visible price and
data-item-price). They must match or Snipcart will block the checkout.

Our Story

A family business, twenty years in the making.

Photo of Mahwish or the store front coming soon

How My Wish began

My Wish by Mahwish Rabbani started as a small, family-run effort to bring the beauty of Pakistani fashion to Long Island. For twenty years, we've hand-selected bridal, party, and casual pieces imported directly from Pakistan — from everyday lawn suits to fully embellished bridal sets.

Every order is treated personally. We're a small team, so when you call or visit, you're talking with the people who actually run the store — not a call center.

What makes us different

We specialize in bridal, party, and casual clothing, along with branded Pakistani designer suits. Beyond ready-made pieces, we also offer in-house tailoring, so your outfit can be adjusted to fit exactly right for your event.

Whether you're shopping for a wedding, an Eid celebration, or something for everyday wear, we're happy to help you find the right piece — and if you don't see it online yet, it's likely already in the store.

Visit us in person

Seeing the fabric and embroidery in person makes a difference — especially for bridal and formal pieces. Stop by to browse, try things on, and talk through tailoring.
